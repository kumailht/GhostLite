

The overall plan is to remove as much from Ghost as possible to make GhostLite which focuses only on blogging.

We will rip out one layer at a time and test along the way.

We don't need almost all features other than blogging (with media uploads & post management), some sort of simple login to for the admin author,
rss, and some basic theming support. The rest can all come out.

We also want the default db to sqlite and sqlite will be a first class citizen, we'll leave mysql as an option for people who want it

We no longer want docker/tests/docs/linting/precommit hooks/really anything that isn't the most simplest setup.

I'll let you drive the ripping out process, while guiding you along the way.

The plan is this: 
- You'll think of the next layer to rip out, I'll say yes or no, and then we move on to the next layer
